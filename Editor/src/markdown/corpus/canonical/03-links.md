An [inline link](https://example.com), one [with a title](https://example.com/page "The title") and one with [**bold** and `code` text](https://example.com/a).

A bare URL stays bare: https://example.com/some_path/file_name.md and so does www.example.com/under_score.

URLs with underscores and queries: https://example.com/a_b_c?first_name=x&last_name=y#frag_ment

A URL at the end of a sentence: https://example.com/path_with_underscores.

In parentheses (https://example.com/a_b) and after a colon: https://example.com/c_d

A link whose text has underscores: [snake_case_name](https://example.com/snake_case_name).

Mail: someone@example.com or [write to us](mailto:team@example.com).

A relative link: [other note](other-note.md), one with spaces: [my file](<notes/my file.md>), one with parentheses: [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markdown_\(disambiguation\)).

*An [italic link](https://example.com/i)* and a [**bold link**](https://example.com/b).

A link glued to text needs brackets: see<https://example.com/x_y>now.
